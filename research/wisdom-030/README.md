# WISDOM 030 — robust preservation under competing futures

Research-only simulator. Python 3.10+, standard library. From this directory set PYTHONPATH=../homeostasis-029:../interdependence-026 (POSIX). API: wisdom.fixture(seed,regime), wisdom.simulate(fixture,policy,lifetime=100000). New policies robust, robust_paced, mean_robust, mean_robust_paced. No live writes or deletion.

One-action selection compares loss of all direct copies under single-peer and paired-domain failures, applied separately to live and advertised inventories. Gains divided by encoded repair cost, then normalized against best feasible action per model. Minimax maximizes minimum normalized positive-model gain; mean variant averages normalized gains. No parity contribution in scoring, but actual ID1 solver verifies recovery. Greedy rather than multistep; hypothetical models not calibrated. Pacing inherited, known 48-tick horizon. Six peers, 6500 B/tick, 100k/160k lifetime ceilings. Costs inherited from HOMEOSTASIS, exclude inventory/transport/common metadata/ingress/filesystem overhead.

900 main runs: 324 training, 576 held-out, nine policies. Seeds 3001–3003 train on six regimes; 3011–3014 held out on eight including new pulse and oscillate. At 100k held-out permanent losses reactive 257, paced 222, robust 266, robust_paced 200. At 160k reactive 134, paced 131, robust 115, robust_paced 127. Training-only worst-regime mean regret selector chooses paced at 100k and reactive at 160k; mean selector chooses paced and robust respectively. Winning variants on test are retrospective, not a prospective policy recommendation. Worst regime-mean regret at 100k paced 4.25, robust_paced 4, reserve 3.75; at 160k reactive 3.75, robust 2.75. Regret against hindsight best of tested policies, not a global oracle.

Fresh posthoc ablation: 192 runs, seeds 3021–3024, attrition/reverse/pulse/oscillate. Permanent losses 100k/160k: reactive 130/46, paced 109/62, robust 147/74, robust_paced 111/69, mean_robust 147/71, mean_robust_paced 110/74. Initial robust improvements did not reliably replicate; no consistent minimax-specific dividend. New policies remain experimental. Simple controls preferred as provisional defaults, not guaranteed best policies.

1092 simulations, 52416 ticks, 114002 hash-verified repair actions, 1132 parity-derived.
